# SpinCore — First Functional Training Specification v1

Status: **CANONICAL FIRST-RELEASE TRAINING CONTRACT**
Date: 2026-09-15

This document answers one question: **what, exactly, is SpinCore training?** It is intentionally operational rather than academic. Any future change to these semantics must be justified as a quality/correctness improvement under the legacy-first policy.

## 1. Game unit and tournament state

SpinCore trains one poker **hand/root state at a time**, not a continuous tournament trajectory from blind 10/20 until a winner is declared. Every root is sampled from the empirically observed SpinGo tournament-state distribution recovered from `Tentativas anteriores de SpinGo.zip`.

This is deliberate: for the WTA chip-EV objective used in v1, the strategically relevant information for the hand is the current domain, cards, positions, blinds, stacks, pot, commitments, legal actions and public action history. Sampling realistic mid-tournament states directly avoids spending compute replaying long sequences merely to reach the same states.

Fixed game contract:

- total chips in tournament: **1500**;
- ante: **0 / not modeled**;
- domains: **THREE_HANDED** and **TRUE_HEADS_UP**;
- normal Hold'em streets: preflop, flop, turn, river;
- terminal training value: exact chip delta, globally scaled by `/1500`;
- first-release payout objective: winner-take-all chip EV;
- money buy-in/stake is not an input: if poker rules are identical, the learned chip strategy is intended to be stake-invariant.

## 2. Domain mixture and opponents

Two separate strategy families are trained: one 3-handed and one true-HU. They do not share a policy brain.

Training budget follows the legacy observed hand prevalence:

- HU: **45.48%** of roots;
- 3-handed: **54.52%** of roots.

Opponents are not fixed scripted bots. Training is **self-play Deep CFR**. At a traverser's decision the legal choices are evaluated to create advantage/regret targets; at opponent decisions an action is sampled from the current behavior strategy. Every live player is used as traverser within a root. Average-policy data are collected from ordinary sampled self-play trajectories after the advantage fit.

There is no opponent-specific exploit model in v1. The target is a strong general baseline strategy.

## 3. Blinds

Nine blind levels are represented. The distributions differ between 3H and HU exactly as in the recovered legacy sampler:

| Blinds | 3H weight | HU weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| 10/20 | 51.91% | 9.07% |
| 15/30 | 29.83% | 24.73% |
| 20/40 | 13.90% | 29.74% |
| 30/60 | 3.74% | 22.61% |
| 40/80 | 0.54% | 10.48% |
| 50/100 | 0.08% | 2.80% |
| 60/120 | 0.01% | 0.49% |
| 80/160 | 0.00% | 0.07% |
| 100/200 | 0.00% | 0.02% |

The complete blind-conditioned empirical stack-bin percentages are canonical in `python/spincore/legacy_scenario_data.json` and are reproduced in the generated DOCX specification. For 3H, forced late 80/160 or 100/200 scenario generation uses the historical 60/120 stack table because those late 3H levels have zero empirical blind weight. HU has explicit distributions through 100/200.

## 4. Stack sampling

### True HU

A stack is drawn from the empirical HU table conditioned on the selected blind. The opponent receives `1500 - sampled_stack`. The two amounts are randomly swapped, one of the three logical seats is randomly dead, the two live players are randomly mapped to the remaining seats, and the dealer is selected among the live seats.

### Three-handed

Three positive stack amounts are independently drawn from the empirical 3H table conditioned on the blind. They are rescaled so the table totals exactly 1500 chips, adjusted for rounding, rejected if invalid/too small for the selected BB, randomly permuted across the three seats, and the dealer is randomly selected.

## 5. Cards and chance sampling

Each root uses a sampled complete deal generated by a root seed. The same root deal is reused when each live player is traversed, reducing irrelevant chance noise between traversers. Different roots receive independent deterministic seeds. Chance is sampled, not exhaustively enumerated.

## 6. Neural state representation

First release uses compact **SPNNIV1 exact-state-derived representation**, not the legacy 292-float vector.

The neural boundary contains:

- 7 card tokens: Hero's two hole cards plus up to five public board cards;
- 16 numeric fields: pot/BB, amount-to-call/BB, current bet/BB, three stacks/BB, three street commitments/BB, three total commitments/BB, SB/BB, a constant feature, blind index and live-player count;
- 8 categorical fields: strategy domain, street, relative dealer/position, live-player count, three player-status categories and visible-board-card count;
- legal-action mask;
- up to 32 public betting-history tokens.

The canonical game state underneath remains exact; compact encoding is only the network interface. Old 292-feature hand-strength/draw/texture knowledge remains available as diagnostic/reference material and is not reintroduced wholesale without evidence of a concrete weakness.

Current model family uses card/categorical embeddings, a numeric trunk, GRU public-history encoder and 10-slot action output carrier. Only seven action slots are active for this baseline. Advantage and AveragePolicy models are separate, and 3H/HU each have their own pair of models.

## 7. Preflop action semantics

The seven historical DeepSpin labels are preserved, but preflop labels are **contextual**; they are not literal pot percentages.

Available strategic semantics:

- `FOLD`;
- `CHECK_CALL` (check where legal, otherwise call; all-in call is not duplicated as a second shove label);
- normal unopened-pot raise: **2.0 BB** (`POT_33` carrier slot);
- isolation after limp(s): **2.5 BB + 1 BB for each extra limper** (`POT_75` slot);
- 3-bet facing one raise: **5.0 BB + 2 BB for each caller after that raise** (`POT_50` slot);
- `ALL_IN` when it is genuine aggression rather than merely an all-in call.

Legacy branch restrictions are preserved:

- a limper facing an isolation does not receive another ordinary-size re-raise branch; the decision collapses toward fold/call/shove;
- after two or more raises, ordinary size families are removed;
- after three or more raises, if Hero still has chips after calling, the flat-call branch is removed and the tree collapses toward fold/shove;
- exact duplicate actions are deduplicated.

The network learns **which** legal action to prefer. The sizes above define the action abstraction; they are not hardcoded strategic decisions.

## 8. Postflop action semantics

Postflop action choices are:

- fold;
- check/call;
- 33% pot;
- 50% pot;
- 75% pot;
- 100% pot;
- all-in.

Fractional sizes are computed on **pot after paying the call**. A fractional raise that is below the legal minimum or cannot be afforded is pruned rather than silently turned into another semantic action. Near-stack fractional bets are collapsed to all-in using the recovered legacy approximately-60%-effective-stack commitment rule, avoiding redundant branches that are strategically equivalent to committing the stack. Exact aliases are deduplicated.

## 9. Deep CFR mechanics

The functional path uses external-sampling Deep CFR with `exact_opponent_levels=0`:

- at traverser nodes: evaluate all legal action branches and create advantage targets;
- at opponent nodes: sample one action from the current behavior policy;
- first untrained behavior is uniform over legal actions;
- once the advantage network is fitted, positive predicted advantages are regret-matched;
- if every legal predicted advantage is non-positive, use a stable masked softmax over the raw advantages instead of the historical bad uniform fallback.

Advantage samples enter a uniform reservoir. Average-policy samples come from independent ordinary sampled trajectories; each encountered decision stores the current behavior distribution and samples one action to continue the hand.

## 10. Objective

The v1 utility is:

`terminal chip delta / 1500`.

Dividing by the constant 1500 is only numerical scaling and cannot change chip-EV action ordering. The legacy `chip_delta / current_BB` target is not used because it changes the relative target magnitude of the same chip value across blind levels in one shared approximator.

State features remain BB-relative where appropriate because stack depth, pot size and amount to call in BB are strategically meaningful.

No separate ICM/payout-specific training is done in v1. The WTA policy is initially reused in multi-place payout games to avoid multiplying compute before the main agent is strong.

## 11. First substantive training budget

Canonical first profile:

- 200 iterations;
- 600 roots/iteration = **120,000 roots**;
- approximately 327 3H + 273 HU roots/iteration;
- every 3H root traverses 3 players; every HU root traverses 2;
- approximately **1527 advantage traversals/iteration**;
- approximately **255 AveragePolicy sampled episodes/iteration** (164 3H + 91 HU);
- advantage reservoir capacity: 100,000;
- policy reservoir capacity: 100,000;
- advantage network reset/re-fit each iteration;
- 50 advantage optimizer steps/iteration;
- batch size 256;
- final AveragePolicy fit: 400 optimizer steps, batch 256;
- durable checkpoint every 5 iterations;
- 5,000 offline self-play hands after finalization.

The 600-root scale is legacy-first: mature DeepSpin used 3 x 512 = 1536 advantage traversals and 256 policy episodes per iteration. The current profile reproduces almost the same per-iteration information scale with the repaired architecture.

## 12. Ryzen execution contract

The user's 32-logical-thread Ryzen is the primary heavy-compute machine. Substantive training must follow `docs/RYZEN_OPTIMIZATION_POLICY.md`.

The original mechanics pilot used two Torch threads and serial root collection, producing ~193% process CPU and therefore only ~6-7% total CPU on a 32-thread machine. That is not the production execution profile.

The optimized path uses a persistent process pool for independent advantage roots. Each root worker is constrained to one Torch/OpenMP/BLAS thread. The parent samples scenarios and owns the authoritative reservoirs; workers return samples that are merged by root order. This changes throughput, not poker semantics.

Before the long run, `tools/benchmark_lean_ryzen_workers.sh` performs one local tuning pass:

1. compares root-worker counts 1/8/16/24/31 and selects useful root throughput;
2. with that worker count fixed, compares parent Torch thread counts 1/2/4/8/16 using warmed iteration time;
3. persists `selected_workers.txt` and `selected_torch_threads.txt`;
4. future substantive/resume scripts consume those selections automatically.

A candidate that fails from oversubscription/memory pressure is skipped rather than aborting the benchmark. High CPU utilization is not itself the objective; the selected configuration is the one that completes useful strategy work fastest and safely.

## 13. What is not being trained in v1

- no opponent-specific exploitation profile;
- no separate payout/ICM brains;
- no monetary stake/buy-in-specific strategy;
- no full legacy 292-feature neural input;
- no fixed-10/20-only global policy;
- no R7.5 dense-referee/certification matrix;
- no multi-month proof/homologation gate chain;
- no live real-money client attachment as part of this training milestone.

## 14. Validation philosophy

Validation is intentionally lean. We keep checks that can catch a real poker/learning/inference bug and remove ceremony that cannot change play quality. The current functional path has already completed offline build/training/inference self-play, and the parallel collector has passed CI with two workers. After substantive training, the first question is practical: did play materially improve relative to the tiny shove/fold-heavy pilot? Only concrete weaknesses justify another architecture change.

## 15. Active-run migration

A serial 120k run started before the Ryzen optimization should not be discarded. Stop after the next printed durable `CHECKPOINT .../checkpoint.pt` line when practical, pull current `main`, execute the one-time Ryzen optimization benchmark, and resume the newest checkpoint with `tools/resume_latest_lean_training_optimized.sh`.

This preserves the work already completed while ensuring the remaining compute uses the optimized Ryzen profile.
