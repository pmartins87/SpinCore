# R7.3 convergence and causal-diagnosis record — 2026-08-10

This document consolidates the physical generation-2 evidence for SpinCore R7.3. It does **not** relax any acceptance gate and does **not** mark the system ready for play.

`READY FOR TABLES = NO`.

## 1. Frozen acceptance contract

R7.3 remains frozen at:

- Advantage weighted normalized RMSE `<= 0.75`
- AveragePolicy weighted mean TV `<= 0.12`
- cross-seed mean TV `<= 0.15`
- cross-seed p95 TV `<= 0.35`

The generation-2 neural architecture currently contains `152,438` trainable parameters per network. Historical R4 evidence recorded `152,434`; the recovered architecture therefore differs by four parameters. No claim of byte-identical recovery is made.

## 2. First generation-2 640-root physical run

The first reconstructed 640-root run exposed two recovery errors:

1. bootstrap behavior used an untrained random AdvantageNet instead of exact zero cumulative regret / uniform legal policy;
2. fixed optimizer-step budgets increasingly underfit the accumulated memories.

The first run consequently failed both individual fit and cross-seed gates:

- mean TV `0.515444`
- p95 TV `0.902261`
- seed 20260829 Advantage NRMSE `0.786415`, policy TV `0.159759`
- seed 20260807 Advantage NRMSE `0.799953`, policy TV `0.176491`

The recovery was corrected by enforcing zero-regret uniform bootstrap, reinitializing AdvantageNet from scratch after every CFR iteration, adaptively fitting the Advantage memory, and fitting AveragePolicy only after CFR collection with a larger bounded optimizer budget.

## 4. 1280-root scale test

Conclusion: brute-force root scaling was stopped. More roots were not addressing the dominant source of instability and increased policy-memory fitting difficulty.

## 5. Controlled variance decomposition

Workflow `31354594794`; evidence commit `efd732288cc0d1414cfe791208fb23433f608be1`.

Two controlled AveragePolicy replicas were trained on each of two independently generated CFR strategy memories. All four individual policy-fit gates passed.

- average disagreement between two replicas trained on the **same** memory: mean TV `0.242653`
- average disagreement when the **memory changed** while optimizer/init seed was held fixed: mean TV `0.469892`
- ratio `1.93647x`

Persisted diagnosis: `CFR_MEMORY_VARIANCE_DOMINANT`.

Important limitation: the within-memory comparisons were initially evaluated on a union corpus containing states from the other memory, so some of the `0.242653` included off-support extrapolation.

## 6. Shared-deck and support-conditioned decomposition

Workflow `31358002704`; evidence commit `a953136d7542465798ff032499708f4e699f75c7`.

The two algorithm seeds were forced to receive the same root hole-card/future-board stream.

- independent-deck across-memory reference mean TV: `0.469892`
- shared-deck across-memory mean TV: `0.460068`
- ratio: `0.97909`

Thus root chance/deck differences explain only a small fraction of the instability.

The support-conditioned result was more informative:

- same-memory replica disagreement on that memory's own support: `0.120310`
- same-memory replica disagreement evaluated on the other memory's support: `0.292305`
- ratio: `2.42959x`

Persisted support diagnosis: `OFF_SUPPORT_POLICY_EXTRAPOLATION_MATERIAL`.

This moved the investigation from generic policy optimization toward why the CFR strategy memories themselves occupy such different supports.

## 7. Exact and card-isomorphic support overlap

Workflow `31360839650`; evidence commit `e4f62f1b9439a31afc4085275d0838828abecaf7`.

The strategy memories were compared directly, independent of neural predictions.

Raw exact observation support:

- Jaccard `0.043432`
- mean LCFR-weight coverage `0.077730`
- weighted shared-target TV `0.538320`

After diagnostic poker-card isomorphism (global suit relabeling plus private-card and flop-card order removal):

- Jaccard `0.029931`
- mean LCFR-weight coverage `0.092190`
- weighted shared-target TV `0.424549`

Card isomorphism modestly improved weighted coverage and target agreement, but did not expand the discrete support intersection and did not make shared CFR targets close. A versioned card-representation rewrite was therefore deferred rather than promoted as the dominant correction.

A key observation from the iteration-level screens is that iteration 1, before any trained AdvantageNet influences behavior, has identical targets on the states that both seeds actually visit but very sparse shared support. This is direct evidence that stochastic path sampling alone can fragment the observed AveragePolicy support even when the underlying behavior policy is the same uniform policy.

## 9. Stronger AdvantageNet fit screen

Workflow `31362660281`; evidence commit `b54171eebe0d314d5df5274ab33d5ace1f183744`.

The corrected generation-2 schedule often stopped Advantage fitting around NRMSE `0.65–0.70`, while the historical pre-loss pilots had achieved roughly `0.45–0.55`. A controlled 256-root screen compared internal fit targets `0.70` and `0.50`.

At iteration 2, the stronger fit produced:

- poker-isomorphic Jaccard `0.036957 -> 0.049105` (`1.32870x`)
- mean LCFR-weight coverage `0.085151 -> 0.108878` (`1.27864x`)
- weighted shared-target TV `0.561440 -> 0.527757` (`0.94001x`)

Persisted diagnosis: `STRONGER_ADVANTAGE_FIT_MATERIAL_AT_SCREEN_SCALE`.

This was the first intervention to show a material improvement in more than one support metric, so it was promoted to a full 640-root candidate rather than immediately changing production.

## 11. Active experiment: external-sampling path-variance decomposition

Workflow `31366433008`.

Rather than take another micro-step, the next diagnostic decomposes both stochastic path estimators in one physical run. It uses 256 unique root deals/seed, a common deck stream, strong Advantage fitting and four modes:

1. `baseline`: 1 Advantage external-sampling trajectory + 1 AveragePolicy own-reach trajectory per unique deal;
2. `strategy_x4`: 1 Advantage trajectory + 4 independent own-reach strategy trajectories;
3. `advantage_x4`: 4 Advantage trajectories + 1 strategy trajectory;
4. `both_x4`: 4 + 4.

Advantage, strategy and optimizer RNG streams are separated **inside this diagnostic only** so the components can be isolated. In particular, baseline and `strategy_x4` have identical advantage traversal/training inputs; the workflow explicitly fails if their Advantage checkpoint NRMSEs diverge beyond numerical tolerance.

Each mode then fits AveragePolicy and reports:

- acceptance-like cross-seed mean/p50/p95/max TV on its own common corpus;
- cross-seed TV on the baseline corpus;
- iteration-specific and cumulative raw/poker-isomorphic support intersection;
- LCFR-weight coverage;
- weighted disagreement between shared strategy targets;
- individual Advantage and AveragePolicy fit gates.

This single experiment is intended to decide whether the next 640-root candidate should spend compute on more own-reach AveragePolicy trajectories, more Advantage external-sampling trajectories, both, or neither.

If none is material, the investigation moves to estimator design (paired/common-random-number or stratified external sampling) rather than further peripheral tuning.

## 12. Current decision state

R7.3 remains **FAIL / ACTIVE**. R7.4 and R8–R12 remain blocked.

The following explanations have been materially narrowed or rejected as dominant causes:

- simple lack of roots;
- root card/chance-stream variation;
- card/suit representation alone;
- training/traversal RNG bookkeeping coupling;
- AveragePolicy optimizer initialization alone;
- insufficient Advantage fit alone.

The dominant unresolved issue is stochastic strategy-memory/path instability under the recovered Deep CFR external-sampling / own-reach collection process.

No acceptance gate has been weakened and no production runtime has been declared ready.
