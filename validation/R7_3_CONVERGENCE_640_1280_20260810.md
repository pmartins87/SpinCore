## Important post-run correction: replicated-candidate V1 was not deck-identical

A line-by-line audit after the three V1 x4 runs found that the V1 candidate runner's hidden-deal formula did **not** match the authoritative generation-2 runner, despite comments/JSON saying that it did.

Authoritative deal schedule:

```text
deck_seed = seed * 1_000_003 + global_root * 97 + iteration
```

where `global_root` is continuous across all CFR iterations.

V1 candidate schedule:

```text
deck_seed = (seed << 32) ^ (iteration << 16) ^ root_index_within_iteration
```

and `root_index` restarted every iteration.

Correction record: `validation/R7_3_REPLICATED_V1_DECK_CONTROL_CORRECTION_20260810.md`.

The V1 physical outputs remain valid independent-deal experiments:
